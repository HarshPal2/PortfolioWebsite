“

How might we help people with diabetes anticipate glucose fluctuations and make more informed decisions?

This question established the guiding direction for the concept design—moving past passive historical monitoring toward proactive, calm anticipation.

Introducing GluSight

GluSight is a concept for predictive glucose monitoring that explores how anticipating potential glucose changes could help people better understand their readings and prepare for what's ahead.

[ Early Concept Exploration ] Interrogating psychological friction around unexpected swings and the need for calm contextual guidance.

[ GluSight System ] Combining real-time telemetry, forward horizon estimates, and physical companion hardware.

Structuring the Experience

The experience brings glucose information and potential future trends together, with the aim of helping users understand their readings and explore what might happen next.

Designing the Experience

The interface explores how current glucose readings, possible future trends, and relevant context could be brought together to support a clearer understanding of glucose information.

SUBSECTION A

Understanding the Present

Current glucose readings provide a snapshot of the user's glucose level. Presenting this information clearly helps users understand their current reading.

SUBSECTION B

Anticipating What's Next

A possible future glucose trend could help users explore how their glucose levels may change over time. These predictions should be presented as estimates rather than certain outcomes.

SUBSECTION C

Understanding the Context

Glucose levels can respond to factors such as meals, physical activity, medication, stress, and sleep. Contextual information could help users explore possible influences on changing trends.

SUBSECTION D

Supporting Informed Decisions

Presenting glucose information in a clearer context could help users consider their next steps. Any guidance should communicate uncertainty appropriately and avoid replacing professional medical advice.

The GluSight Experience

GluSight explores how glucose readings and potential future trends could be presented in one experience, helping users understand the information available to them and consider what may happen next.

1. Current Telemetry & Reading Status

Displays real-time blood glucose alongside velocity trend arrows and timestamped sensor connectivity.

Important Interaction: Glanceable hero typography gives instant confirmation of current glycemic state without requiring navigation through menus.

2. Calibrated Forward Forecast

Visualizes estimated trajectory 20 minutes in advance with shaded target bands and explicit model confidence indicators.

Important Interaction: Predictions are visualized as probabilistic corridors rather than rigid outcomes, preventing false certainty and alarm fatigue.

3. Frictionless Context Capture

Enables rapid tagging of meals and physical activity to help users correlate everyday habits with observed glucose swings.

Important Interaction: One-tap category chips (Sugary, Heavy Meal, Light Meal) eliminate cumbersome text entry during daily routines.

4. Adaptive Alert Thresholds

Customizes predictive high and low alert sensitivities tailored to the individual's diabetes classification and clinical targets.

Important Interaction: Smart silent mode and sensitivity calibration prevent intrusive false alarms while preserving critical warnings.

Looking Beyond Monitoring

GluSight explores how predictive glucose insights could extend the monitoring experience beyond current readings. Further work would focus on evaluating how users understand predictions, interpret uncertainty, and use these insights in everyday life.
