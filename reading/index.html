Case study: GluSight

What if your glucose monitor could tell you what happens next?

Living with diabetes means more than keeping track of numbers. GluSight is a design concept that explores how presenting current glucose information alongside a possible future trend could help people anticipate changes and better understand what they are seeing.

  • Current glucose Present reading and its trend
  • Predicted trend A possible future, shown as an estimate
  • Communicating uncertainty Observed vs estimated values
  • Contextual information Meals, activity, medication, stress, sleep

GluSight is an independent concept and has not been clinically validated. The full write-up is in the Kitchen of the 3D house: read the full case study.
